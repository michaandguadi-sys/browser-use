## Q1. Life-domain frameworks and the domains that matter for nomads (incl. verified legal/tax/border facts)

### Takeaway
The compliance-heavy domains carry hard dates and large penalties, so the agent should treat them as deterministic, calendar-driven jobs, not as LLM judgement calls. These are US LLC filings (Form 5472 plus pro forma 1120, Wyoming annual report), German tax residence (§8/§9 AO, §2 AStG) and Schengen 90/180, which EES now enforces biometrically. The softer domains (craft, relationships, rest) suit low-frequency digests better.

### Cited Findings

**US LLC compliance (foreign-owned single-member LLC)**
- A foreign-owned US disregarded entity counts as a "reporting corporation". It must file a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 attached by the Form 1120 due date, including extensions. Name and address are the only Form 1120 information required. — [IRS Instructions for Form 5472 (Rev. Dec 2024)](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i5472.pdf)
- The penalty is $25,000 for each failure to file a complete and correct Form 5472 on time, or to keep the required records. A "substantially incomplete" form counts as a failure to file. — [IRS i5472](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i5472.pdf); [IRS International information reporting penalties](https://www.irs.gov/payments/international-information-reporting-penalties)
- A continuation penalty of $25,000 applies for each 30-day period after a 90-day window that starts with an IRS notice, with no maximum. Reasonable-cause relief exists. Penalties may be systemically assessed when a 5472 is attached to a late-filed 1120. — [IRS penalties page](https://www.irs.gov/payments/international-information-reporting-penalties); [IRM 20.1.9](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part20/irm_20-001-009); [IRM 8.11.5](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part8/irm_08-011-005)
- The $25,000 amount applies to tax years beginning on or after 1 Jan 2018; earlier years used $10,000. — [IRS i5472 2018 rev.](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i5472--2018.pdf)
- **Wyoming annual report:** the license tax is the greater of $60 or $0.0002 per dollar of Wyoming-located assets, so the $60 minimum applies under $300k of assets. It is due on the first day of the LLC's formation-anniversary month. The LLC becomes delinquent the day after the due-date month ends; one source says it is dissolved about 60 days after the due date and that reinstatement costs $100 plus the reports owed. — [Nolo](https://nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/annual-report-tax-filing-requirements-wyoming-llcs.html); [UpCounsel](https://upcounsel.com/wyoming-annual-report); [businessanywhere.io](https://businessanywhere.io/wyoming-llc-annual-report/). **Secondary sources only. They disagree on how early you can file (120 days vs. one year) and on the reinstatement window. Verify on wyobiz.wyo.gov (Wyoming Secretary of State); not verified here.**

**German tax residence and leaving Germany (Wegzug)**
- Unlimited German tax liability attaches first to a *Wohnsitz* (§8 AO): any dwelling kept under circumstances that suggest it will be kept and used. A key to an empty flat can suffice. Deregistering (Abmeldung) is only an indication, not decisive. — [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/steuerpflicht/); [it-daily](https://www.it-daily.net/?p=151687); [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Wohnsitzwechsel-und-beschraenkte-Steuerpflicht.html)
- *Gewöhnlicher Aufenthalt* (§9 AO) means a continuous stay of more than six months, and short interruptions do not break it. Purely private stays of up to one year are excluded. "183 days" is a rule of thumb, not the statutory test. — [wohnsitzausland.com](https://www.wohnsitzausland.com/steuerwissen/gewoehnlicher-aufenthalt-183-tage-regel/); [Finanztip](https://www.finanztip.de/steuerpflicht/)
- With no German Wohnsitz or habitual abode, limited liability (§49 EStG) still covers German-source income. Extended limited liability (§2 AStG) can apply for 10 years after leaving. A double tax treaty (DBA) allocates taxing rights but does not end national liability. — [steuerschroeder.de](https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Wohnsitzwechsel-und-beschraenkte-Steuerpflicht.html)
- **Caveat:** these are guide and law-firm pages, not BZSt or BMF primary texts. The §6 AStG exit tax on ≥1% company shareholdings was not researched (see Gaps).

**Schengen and border systems**
- EES (the biometric Entry/Exit System) began a phased rollout on 12 Oct 2025 and has been fully operational since 10 April 2026. It replaces manual passport stamping. — [French MFA (diplomatie.gouv.fr)](https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/en/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/ees-le-nouveau-systeme-europeen-de-gestion-des-frontieres-entre-en-service-le-10-avril-2026); [EU Home Affairs Smart Borders](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/smart-borders_nl)
- ETIAS is expected to start in Q4 2026. It applies to visa-exempt third-country nationals for short stays of 90 days in any 180-day period. — [eiglaw.com](https://eiglaw.com/eu-entry-exit-system-goes-live-on-april-10-2026/); [The Local](https://www.thelocal.com/20250722/eu-reveals-latest-start-date-for-ees-biometric-passport-checks)
- **Important inference for this user:** both systems apply to third-country nationals. As EU (German) citizens, the couple is not subject to Schengen 90/180, EES or ETIAS. Those rules only matter for a non-EU partner, for collaborators and crew, or by analogy for other countries' day limits (e.g. UK, Thailand, Georgia). The "Schengen day 80/90" use case is therefore only valid if one partner is non-EU. **Confirm the partner's nationality.**

**Drones (EU, EASA framework)**
- The Open category requires MTOM under 25 kg, visual line of sight (VLOS) and a 120 m height limit. Its subcategories are:
  - A1: over people but not assemblies, with C0/C1 drones.
  - A2: close to uninvolved people, with a C2 drone and the A2 certificate. Legacy drones have been barred from A2 since 1 Jan 2024.
  - A3: at least 150 m from residential, commercial, industrial or recreational areas.
  — [Norwegian CAA (Luftfartstilsynet)](https://luftfartstilsynet.no/en/drones/veiledning/open-category/); [Estonian Transport Administration](https://www.transpordiamet.ee/en/open-category-flights); [Malta Transport](https://www.transport.gov.mt/Aviation/Drones/Information-Help-4445)
- Operator registration is required for drones of 250 g or more, or with a camera or other personal-data sensor. The registration number must be shown on the drone. Insurance is required; Norway cites at least 0.75 M SDR of third-party cover. Flights must respect national UAS geographical zones. — [Norwegian CAA](https://luftfartstilsynet.no/en/drones/veiledning/open-category/)

**Frameworks for structuring domains**
- No primary sources were retrieved for Wheel of Life, PARA, GTD, OKRs, Newport or Huberman in this session (see Gaps). The domain list below is a design synthesis.

### Inferences
**Proposed domain map (13 domains)** and concrete proactive use cases, written as trigger → action → surface. Surfaces: G = glasses (≤10 words, glanceable), P = phone push, L = laptop/desktop card, D = daily or weekly digest. The legal anchors are cited above; the use cases themselves are design proposals.

1. **Clients and brand deals**
   - Invoice unpaid at due + 7 days → draft a polite reminder in the client's language and queue it for approval → L (or D if not urgent).
   - Brand-pitch email unanswered after 5 business days → draft a follow-up with a fresh hook (latest video stats) → D.
   - Contract PDF received → extract usage rights, exclusivity, deliverables and deadlines into the project tracker, and flag clauses outside your norms (perpetual rights, unpaid revisions) → L.
   - Deliverable due in 48 h with no file in the "final" folder → P.
   - Inbound sponsorship inquiry → enrich it (company, budget signals, past creator deals) and score fit against channel values → D.
2. **Content production (YouTube)**
   - Upload scheduled in under 24 h with missing thumbnail, title, chapters or captions → checklist card → L.
   - First 24–48 h of a new video: CTR or retention below the channel median → suggest a title or thumbnail A/B swap → P, once only.
   - Comment spike or a negative-sentiment cluster → digest of the top themes → D.
   - Footage ingest finished → check backups (3-2-1), confirm checksums, log the shot list → L.
   - Weekly: the publish cadence slips against plan → raise it in the weekly review → D.
3. **Finances and taxes**
   - Wyoming annual report: alerts at T−60, T−30 and T−7 days before the 1st of the anniversary month → D, then P at T−7. The registered-agent renewal follows the same pattern.
   - Form 5472 + pro forma 1120 for the LLC: start gathering reportable transactions in January, alert at T−45 and T−14 before 15 April (or the extended date) → D, then P. Escalate hard, because the penalty is $25k.
   - Every LLC↔owner money movement (contributions, distributions, owner-paid expenses) is a 5472 "reportable transaction" → auto-tag it as it happens → silent log, monthly D. This is an inference from the 5472 reporting duty; confirm scope with the instructions or an advisor.
   - German side: monitor any indicator that could create a German Wohnsitz (keeping a flat or keys, a long stay) → quarterly D.
   - Wise balances, FX moves or a large incoming payment → suggest conversion timing and set-aside for tax → D.
   - Subscription audit (software, cloud, insurance) → monthly D.
4. **Borders, visas and day counts**
   - Per-country rolling day counter, driven by phone location and border crossings.
   - At about 75% of any limit (Schengen 90/180 for a non-EU partner; UK, Thailand, US ESTA or other limits for the couple) → warn and propose exit routes or visa-run options → P. Escalate again at 90% → P + G.
   - Passport expires within 9 months, or fewer than 2–4 blank pages → D.
   - ETIAS or eVisa needed for a non-EU collaborator or partner on a planned route → D.
   - Day ≥150 in any single country → flag the risk of tax residence or habitual abode (by analogy to §9 AO; many states use 183 days) → D.
5. **Health and insurance**
   - Arrival in a new country → check vaccination and health advisories (Auswärtiges Amt health leaflets via its OpenData API), list the nearest hospital, check that the travel or health insurance covers the country (many exclude the US) → P on arrival, then D.
   - Insurance policy renewal → T−30 D.
   - Sleep or jet-lag management after flights crossing ≥4 time zones → schedule a light-exposure suggestion → P, morning only.
   - Medication refill based on days of supply remaining → D.
6. **Safety**
   - Auswärtiges Amt travel warning changes for the current or next country (poll the OpenData `travelwarning` endpoint and diff) → P immediately for the current country, D for the planned ones.
   - Severe weather or wildfire alert at the location → P + G.
   - Solo shoot in remote terrain → dead-man check-in: no "I'm OK" by time T → escalate to the partner, then an emergency contact. This is an explicit opt-in feature.
7. **Gear and logistics**
   - Flight in under 48 h → battery rules (Wh limits), carry-on weight for gear, a customs carnet reminder for expensive kit → D, then P at T−24 h.
   - Gear insurance and serial-number inventory kept current → quarterly D.
   - Drone firmware or app update required before a shoot → L.
   - Courier tracking for gear shipped ahead → P only on exceptions.
8. **Location scouting and shoot planning**
   - Golden or blue hour in 40 minutes at the current or planned location, and cloud cover is suitable → G + P. Sun position via a suncalc-type library and cloud forecast via Open-Meteo.
   - Planned drone shot location falls in a UAS geographical zone (airport, national park, military) → warn and link the national zone map → L at planning time, P the day before.
   - Wind or gust at the shot time is above the drone's limit → P the evening before.
   - Permit lead times (national parks, city filming permits) against the shoot date → D at T−30.
   - Moon phase or Milky Way window for night shots → D.
9. **Relationships and the couple as co-workers**
   - The weekly couple sync (business agenda separate from personal) is auto-assembled from open decisions → D.
   - Protected non-work time is being eaten by shoots for 2+ weeks → gentle flag to both → D. Never mid-shoot.
   - Birthdays and anniversaries of family in Germany and of key network contacts → D at T−7.
   - Time-zone-aware "good time to call family" window → D.
10. **Learning and craft**
    - Monthly skill goal (e.g. colour grading, expedition safety training, drone A2 certificate) tracked → D.
    - Relevant festival, grant or broadcaster call for expedition documentaries (deadline at T−30) → D.
    - After each project, a short retro prompt → D.
11. **Community and network**
    - Another filmmaker or creator from your network is in, or arriving in, your city → D.
    - Relationship-decay alert: a key contact (commissioner, broadcaster, brand manager) not contacted for 90 days → draft a touchpoint → D.
    - Collab opportunity surfaced from comments or email → D.
12. **Rest, burnout and energy**
    - Consecutive days without a day off ≥10, or late-night editing after 23:00 ≥4 nights a week → D, plus one P.
    - Travel-day density (≥3 transit days a week) → suggest a slow week → D.
    - In this domain the agent should mostly suppress other nudges (protecting the rest window).
13. **Admin and life-support**
    - Address, mail scanning and bank-security device checks, domain renewals, 2FA backup codes, ID document renewals → D.

**Design rule from this mapping:** compliance and safety items with legal or physical consequences get deterministic schedulers with escalation ladders. Everything else is LLM-ranked and batched into digests.

## Q2. Science of WHEN a proactive assistant should speak: interruptibility, receptivity, JITAI, proactive-agent benchmarks

### Takeaway
The best-supported model is an expected-utility gate. Interrupt only when value(now) minus the cost of interrupting exceeds value(deferred), following Horvitz's mixed-initiative principles. Notification attention is already saturated (a median of 56 notifications a day), and receptivity is predictable, with about 70% sensitivity and 80% specificity in one study. So the agent should be batch-first, interrupt rarely, and learn timing per user, with timing treated as a separate decision from content (ProAgentBench).

### Cited Findings
- **Interruption cost:** in Gloria Mark's field data, 81.9% of interrupted work was resumed the same day, after an average of 23 min 15 s, with about two intervening tasks before returning. This measures time to resume, not cognitive recovery time, and is often misquoted. — [Gallup Business Journal interview with Gloria Mark (2006)](https://news.gallup.com/businessjournal/23146/too-many-interruptions-work.aspx); caveats in [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/t-work-interruptions-23-minutes-275-pings-organisational-focus/)
- **Notification load:**
  - 15 users over one week averaged 63.5 notifications a day, viewed within minutes even in silent mode. — [Pielot et al., MobileHCI 2014](https://ic.unicamp.br/~oliveira/doc/MHCI2014_An-in-situ-study-of-mobile-phone-notifications.pdf)
  - 794,525 notifications from 278 users gave a median of 56 a day. Non-messaging notifications rarely convert (about 15–25%). — [Pielot, Vradi, Park, MobileHCI 2018](https://www.interruptions.net/literature/Pielot-MobileHCI18.pdf)
- **Receptivity is predictable:** about 70,000 notifications from 35 users. Receptivity was predicted with 70% sensitivity and 80% specificity, and the learned model beat the rules users wrote about their own interruptibility. — [Mehrotra & Musolesi, *Intelligent Notification Systems* survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.10171)
- Cognitive and physical context (task complexity, activity) affects both response time and perceived disruption. — ["My Phone and Me", Mehrotra et al., CHI 2016](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1502364/)
- **JITAI framework (Nahum-Shani et al.):** six components — distal outcome, proximal outcome, decision points, intervention options, tailoring variables, decision rules. Receptivity can act as a tailoring variable, and "deliver nothing" or "postpone" are valid intervention options. — [Künzler et al., *Detecting Receptivity for mHealth Interventions* (arXiv 2011.08302)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.08302); framework origin [Nahum-Shani et al. via PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4732268)
- Receptivity can be split into acceptance (engaging with the prompt) and feasibility (being able to act, given the situation). — [Real-World Receptivity to Adaptive Mental Health Interventions (arXiv 2508.02817)](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02817v1)
- A 2025 review found JITAI decision rules and decision points often lack empirical support, and that adaptivity and receptivity are frequently missing. — [Frontiers in Digital Health 2025](https://doaj.org/article/510e1bbe2bdc4f46bb8acec7895e6da3)
- **Horvitz mixed-initiative principles (CHI 1999):**
  - Act autonomously only when the expected value of acting exceeds that of not acting.
  - Weigh the cost of deferring against the cost of interrupting.
  - Use dialogue to resolve key uncertainty about the user's goals.
  - The named failure modes are poor goal guessing, weak cost–benefit reasoning and poor timing.
  - The BusyBody work learns a user's cost of interruption from their context.
  — [Horvitz CHI'99 PDF](https://www.erichorvitz.com/chi99horvitz.pdf); [Principles page](https://erichorvitz.com/uiact.htm); [BusyBody CSCW](https://www.erichorvitz.com/busybody_cscw.pdf); [Cambridge IWML lecture](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/teaching/1819/P230/IWML-Lecture-3.pdf)
- **Proactive LLM assistants (CHI 2025):** in a randomized study of a proactive programming assistant, the authors report significant benefits with nuances. Their design principles are that suggestions should be quick to evaluate, easy to act on and dismissible, and that untimely or irrelevant proactive messages compromise the interaction. — [Chen et al., "Need Help? Designing Proactive AI Assistants for Programming", CHI 2025 (arXiv 2410.04596)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04596); [Microsoft Research page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/need-help-designing-proactive-ai-assistants-for-programming/)
- **ProAgentBench (arXiv 2602.04482, 2026):** splits proactive assistance into a *timing* decision ("when to help") and *content* generation, using real user sessions. Long-term memory and historical context improve timing prediction. — [ProAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04482v1)
- A 2026 theory paper calls ProAgentBench's precision metric a proxy for interruption cost. — [arXiv 2609.07741](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.07741)
- A 2026 paper notes that no benchmark yet scores monitoring, relevance, timing, framing and silence together. It also cautions that interruption findings from real-time work may not transfer to asynchronous agent messages. — [arXiv 2605.06717 "Agentic Coding Needs Proactivity, Not Just Autonomy"](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06717)
- ProactiveBench (Lu et al., 2024) is cited as suggestion-focused prior work, alongside ContextAgent and ProAgent. — cited in [arXiv 2605.06717](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.06717)
- For multimodal proactive assistance on streaming video (relevant to glasses), see IPIBench. — [Emergent Mind summary](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/ipibench) (aggregator; primary paper not opened)

### Inferences
**"Right time, right channel, right size" rules for the Life OS**, derived from the findings above:

1. **Expected-utility gate (Horvitz).** Score each candidate nudge as `U = P(relevant) × value × urgency_decay(t) − C_interrupt(context, channel)`. Deliver now only if U(now) > U(next batch slot). Otherwise defer to a digest. Silence is a first-class action (JITAI "deliver nothing").
2. **Separate timing from content (ProAgentBench).** One model or rule set decides *whether and when*. The LLM only writes *what* after the gate passes. This prevents eloquent but untimely nudges.
3. **Urgency tiers mapped to channels:**
   - **T0 Safety or legal hard deadline** (travel warning for the current country, severe weather, day limit ≥90%, a filing due in ≤7 days unfiled): immediate P, plus G if worn. May break DND.
   - **T1 Time-boxed opportunity** (golden hour in 40 min, wind window, client asks "today?"): P or G, but only if a receptivity check passes (not driving, not mid-take, not in a meeting). Otherwise drop it, since it is perishable.
   - **T2 Actionable, not perishable** (invoice reminder draft, pitch follow-up): laptop card at the next natural breakpoint (end of an editing session or calendar gap), or the daily digest.
   - **T3 Awareness** (stats, network, learning): weekly digest only.
4. **Receptivity signals** (tailoring variables): calendar (block types such as "shoot", "edit-deep", "travel"), motion (driving or flying = defer), location dwell (just arrived = good for arrival briefings), device state (screen unlocked, app switch = a breakpoint), local time and sleep window, and recent nudge count. Learn per user, because Mehrotra found learned models beat users' self-described rules.
5. **Budget the attention.** Against a background of about 56 notifications a day, cap proactive pushes at roughly 3 a day per person (excluding T0), and glasses at about 1–2 a day. These caps are a design choice, not empirically derived. Re-tune them from the useful-rate metric in Q4.
6. **Interrupt at breakpoints, not mid-task.** Prefer task boundaries (render started, upload done, calendar gap) to protect deep work. Mark's data says resumption takes about 23 min with about two intervening tasks.
7. **Size to the surface.** Glasses: ≤10 words plus one action ("Golden hour 40 min, clear W"). Phone: a title, one line, and 1–2 action buttons (Approve draft / Snooze). Laptop: a full card with draft and context. Digest: ranked sections with "why you're seeing this". Make every nudge dismissible, quick to evaluate and one tap to act on (Chen et al. CHI 2025).
8. **Explain and ask once (Horvitz dialogue principle).** When uncertain (P(relevant) between 0.4 and 0.7), ask a single clarifying question in the digest rather than pushing.
9. **Couple-aware routing.** Send each item to the partner who owns that domain, so both are not interrupted. Reserve both-partner pushes for T0.

## Q3. Data APIs and sources for triggers (availability and cost)

### Takeaway
Most core triggers can be built on free or near-free sources: Open-Meteo for weather (free for non-commercial use only, under 10k calls a day), suncalc-style local maths for sun position, the Auswärtiges Amt OpenData travel-warning endpoint (free, ID-based), and Healthchecks.io for monitoring. Because the user runs a commercial channel, Open-Meteo's commercial licence or self-hosting is the compliant path. Drone-zone data is the weakest link: no single verified API was found, so it must be handled country by country.

### Cited Findings
- **Open-Meteo:**
  - The free API is for non-commercial use, under 10,000 calls a day, 5,000 an hour and 600 a minute. — [Open-Meteo Terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)
  - A commercial licence is a subscription with an API key and dedicated servers, not billed per call. — [Open-Meteo Pricing](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing)
  - Self-hosting under AGPLv3 gives practically unlimited calls. Data is CC BY 4.0 and needs attribution. — [open-meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/)
  - Exact commercial prices were not retrieved, and a third-party "300K/month" claim conflicts with the official terms. — [Geekflare](https://geekflare.com/guides/weather-api/)
- **Auswärtiges Amt OpenData:**
  - Official interface covering travel and safety advice, German missions abroad, foreign missions in Germany, health leaflets and country names. — [AA Open-Data-Schnittstelle](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/service/opendata/open-data-schnittstelle-736118)
  - Since Dec 2021, `/opendata/travelwarning` returns a list of IDs, with details at `/opendata/travelwarning/{ID}`. ISO-3 codes were added in Aug 2022.
  - Terms require full, current reuse with attribution, and access can be revoked.
  - An unofficial Python client exists, `de-travelwarning`; it notes that errors and deactivation return `{}`. — [PyPI de-travelwarning](https://pypi.org/project/de-travelwarning/)
  - Inference: treat an empty `{}` as an *error*, not as "no warnings", for the reliability reasons in Q4.
- **Monitoring:**
  - Healthchecks.io's free tier covers 20 checks.
  - Each check has a Period and a Grace; a check is "Late" after the Period and "Down" after Period + Grace, which triggers an alert.
  - Cron-expression schedules are supported, and the BSD-licensed code can be self-hosted.
  — [Healthchecks.io](https://HealthChecks.io); [freetier.co](https://freetier.co/directory/products/healthchecksio)
- **Observability:** Langfuse is MIT-licensed and self-hostable. The Cloud Hobby tier is free at 50k units a month for 2 users. It traces agent workflows and runs LLM-as-judge or code evaluators on live traffic. — [Langfuse clarifications (vendor)](https://langfuse.com/resources/engineering/clarifications); [Langfuse online evals docs](https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/get-started/online.md)

### Inferences
**Trigger-source matrix.** Items marked "unverified" were not checked this session.
- Weather, cloud cover, wind: Open-Meteo (commercial licence or self-hosted for a monetized channel).
- Golden or blue hour: compute locally (the suncalc algorithm). No API, no cost, works offline.
- Travel safety: poll AA OpenData every 6–12 h and diff the `lastModified` or content hash per country ID.
- Border day counts: derive from phone location history plus manual border-crossing confirmation. No official API (the EU's own short-stay calculator is a web tool; unverified as an API).
- Drone zones: national authority maps or apps per country (EASA framework, national implementation). Third-party aggregators (UAV Forecast, DJI FlySafe/GEO zones) are unverified for API access and terms.
- Finance: the Wise API for balances and transfer webhooks (unverified this session). Treat it as read-only for the agent, with no outbound payments without human approval.
- Email and calendar: Gmail and Google Calendar APIs. MCP connectors are already available in this environment (observed tool list), so no extra API work is needed for drafts and event reads.
- YouTube: the Data API and Analytics API, on a quota-unit model (unverified). Use them for the post-publish CTR and retention checks.
- Notion API for the tracker databases (unverified on pricing and limits).

## Q4. Reliability and evaluation for a 24/7 personal agent

### Takeaway
Run the agent like a small production service. That means three layers: deterministic schedulers backed by idempotent jobs and dead-man's-switch heartbeats (Healthchecks.io's Period + Grace model); full trace observability with online LLM-as-judge plus code evals (Langfuse); and a small set of personal SLOs, chiefly "no missed T0/legal deadline", plus a measured *precision of proactive nudges* (useful-interruption rate) that feeds a thumbs-up/down loop into the timing rules.

### Cited Findings
- Dead-man's-switch semantics: a job pings a URL on completion. If no ping arrives within Period + Grace, the check goes "Down" and alerts. Grace should be set a little above the job's expected duration. — [Healthchecks.io](https://HealthChecks.io); [freetier.co listing](https://freetier.co/directory/products/healthchecksio)
- Langfuse supports online LLM-as-judge and code evaluators via rules on incoming observations. Scores can be numeric, categorical or boolean. Scores from your own external eval code can be written back to traces. A self-hosted setup needs web, worker, Postgres, Redis and ClickHouse, plus a model connection for the judge. — [Langfuse online eval docs](https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/get-started/online.md); [Langfuse model-based evals](https://langfuse.com/docs/scores/model-based-evals); [Railway template](https://railway.com/deploy/langfuse-evals--deploy-langfuse-llm-as-a-judge-evals)
- Precision is already used as the proxy for interruption cost in proactive-agent benchmarking. — [arXiv 2609.07741](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.07741) on [ProAgentBench](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04482v1)
- Learned receptivity models beat users' self-described rules, which supports learning from feedback over hand-coded rules alone. — [Mehrotra & Musolesi survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.10171)
- An unofficial AA OpenData client returns `{}` on error or deactivation, a concrete silent-failure mode. — [PyPI de-travelwarning](https://pypi.org/project/de-travelwarning/)

### Inferences
**Reliability architecture**
1. **Two-plane design.** A *deterministic plane* (cron or queue jobs: deadline ladders, day counters, API polls) must never depend on an LLM to fire. An *LLM plane* (ranking, drafting, summarizing) can fail soft: when it fails, deliver the raw deterministic alert with a template.
2. **Idempotent jobs.**
   - Each nudge gets a stable key `hash(domain, entity_id, trigger_type, window)`, stored in a "sent" ledger, so retries and restarts never double-notify.
   - Drafts are created, never sent, unless there is explicit approval.
   - Uses a uuid7 id per event; uuid7 suits time-ordered logs.
3. **Retries with backoff and jitter** for external APIs. Classify each failure as transient or permanent. Treat an empty or schema-invalid response as a failure, not as "no news" (the AA `{}` case). Add a circuit breaker per source that raises a "source degraded" item into the digest.
4. **Heartbeats and dead-man's switches.**
   - Every scheduled job pings its own Healthchecks check, which is free for up to 20 checks.
   - Add a top-level "agent alive" check pinged every 15–60 min, routed to a *different* channel (e.g. email or SMS) from the agent's own push channel, so a dead agent can't silence its own alarm.
   - The agent should also run an inverse watchdog: the digest itself must arrive by 08:00 local, or an external check fires.
5. **Time-zone correctness.**
   - Store all deadlines in UTC with the jurisdiction's legal TZ attached (US Eastern for the IRS, Mountain for Wyoming).
   - Compute delivery windows in the user's current TZ.
   - Nomads cross TZs constantly, which is a classic source of off-by-one-day misses. Add tests for date-line and DST crossings.
6. **Offline and degraded connectivity.**
   - Cache tomorrow's sun times, forecasts and permits locally on the phone, because remote shoots lose signal.
   - Queue outbound items, and mark them stale with a TTL; a golden-hour nudge after sunset is worse than none.

**Personal SLOs (proposed)**
- **SLO-1 (legal and safety):** 100% of T0 deadlines alerted at ≥2 points on the escalation ladder before the due date. Error budget: 0. Any miss triggers a postmortem.
- **SLO-2 (freshness):** safety sources (AA, weather alerts) polled successfully at least once every 12 h, 99% of the time.
- **SLO-3 (digest availability):** daily digest delivered by 08:00 local on ≥99% of days. Weekly review prepared by Sunday 18:00.
- **SLO-4 (nudge precision):** useful-interruption rate (pushes marked useful or acted on within the TTL ÷ all pushes) ≥60% over a rolling 14 days. Annoyance rate (explicit 👎 or "don't show this again") ≤10%. These targets are design choices, chosen to exceed the roughly 15–25% conversion of generic notifications reported by Pielot 2018.
- **SLO-5 (latency for T1):** perishable alerts (golden hour, wind window) delivered with ≥30 min lead time ≥95% of the time.

**Evaluation stack**
- **Golden test set:** about 50–200 frozen scenarios, each a context snapshot plus the expected decision (push / digest / silent, and which partner), covering every domain. Include *negative* cases where the right answer is silence. Run on every prompt, model or rule change, as a regression gate in CI.
- **Online evals in Langfuse:** an LLM judge scores each delivered nudge on relevance, correct tier, size or format compliance (glasses ≤10 words) and factual grounding (links to source). Code evals check hard constraints such as no outbound payment, no email sent without approval, and deadline arithmetic.
- **Feedback loop:**
  - Each nudge carries 👍/👎/"not now"/"never for this". 👎 and "never" write a suppression rule (domain × trigger × context).
  - "Not now" teaches a timing preference (the receptivity model).
  - Make rules human-readable and editable, because Horvitz recommends dialogue under uncertainty and editable rules support trust.
- **Weekly self-audit (GTD-style weekly review, applied to the agent):**
  - Report on all SLOs, the top 5 misfires (👎) with root cause, sources degraded, and deadlines coming in the next 90 days.
  - Propose rule changes for one-tap human approval.
  - A missed SLO-1 forces a written postmortem (blameless, SRE-style).
- **Canaries:** a synthetic "fake deadline" injected weekly must surface on schedule. This tests the full path from scheduler to ranking to delivery.
- **Change management:** version prompts and rules, roll out new nudge types in shadow mode first (log only, judge-scored, not delivered), and promote once shadow precision is ≥ the target.

### Gaps
- LangSmith's features and pricing were not researched. The comparison found ([Leanware Langfuse vs LangSmith](https://leanware.co/insights/langfuse-vs-langsmith)) was not opened.
- No published SRE guidance specific to *personal* agents was found. The SLO framing is an adaptation of general SRE practice (Google SRE book not fetched this session).
- No empirical benchmarks were found for acceptable precision or annoyance thresholds in personal proactive agents. The proposed targets are heuristic.
- The reliability of LLM-as-judge for timing decisions specifically (agreement with human labels) was not researched.
